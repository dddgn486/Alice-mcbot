## Blocking findings

1. **Abort releases the in-transit/suspended replacement protection while inventory is intentionally unchanged.** `transferAbort` permits an `IN_TRANSIT_BOT` or `SUSPENDED` entry and changes it to `ABORTED`, preserving only its current location (`src/main/java/com/dddgn/alice/command/BotCommand.java:172-186`). It does not cancel or retain a special protected state for the active task. The sole generic ledger guard only blocks `IN_TRANSIT_BOT` or `SUSPENDED` (`src/main/java/com/dddgn/alice/transfer/TransferLedgerData.java:91-95`; `src/main/java/com/dddgn/alice/bot/BotManager.java:426-445`). Thus immediately after abort, a normal `assign`, soft probe, place, or road assignment can pass the guard and replace the TransferTask even when the entry location remains `BOT_INVENTORY` (`BotManager.java:448-501`). This is precisely the protected-item-location escape requested for review. It conflicts with the plan's required protection for in-transit/suspended ownership (`active-plan.md:53-55`) and HANDOVER's statement that a persistent in-transit/suspended entry blocks unrelated replacement (`docs/HANDOVER.md:59`).

2. **One ordinary replacement route bypasses the guard even before abort.** `BotSession.assignFollow` calls `replaceTaskIfRunning()` but ignores its `false` result, then creates a FollowTask regardless (`src/main/java/com/dddgn/alice/bot/BotManager.java:465-470`). When ledger state is `IN_TRANSIT_BOT` or `SUSPENDED`, the prior TransferTask is not cleared, but `beginTask` overwrites `task`; this loses the active transfer task without an allowed replacement. The committed line directly violates the plan's requirement that normal replacement be refused and not alter unrelated task semantics (`active-plan.md:54`; implementation route `original-chest-bot-transfer-a1-implementation-plan-20260821.md:173-177`).

3. **The active deadline records the wrong proven location after source extraction.** The 2,400-tick branch always calls `suspend(..., NOT_MOVED)` (`src/main/java/com/dddgn/alice/task/TransferTask.java:39-41`), including `TO_DESTINATION` and `DESTINATION_WRITE`, even though source delta has already been persisted as `IN_TRANSIT_BOT/BOT_INVENTORY` (`TransferTask.java:65-66`). A deadline expiry after extraction therefore overwrites the last proven location as `NOT_MOVED`, contradicting the required location ledger and the plan's in-transit policy (`active-plan.md:50-53`).

4. **Focused fixture coverage does not meet the approved matrix, and HANDOVER overstates it.** The fixture invokes primitives directly for normal legs and does not execute `TransferTask` end-to-end (`src/main/java/com/dddgn/alice/transfer/TransferFixture.java:49-65`). It directly calls `ledger.transition` to model timeout, restart, abort, and replacement rather than exercising command, manager, task state, elapsed tick budgets, or replacement entry points (`TransferFixture.java:163-187`). It has no 200/2,400/12,000 tick progression assertion; no real `HARD_PATH` search-limit/unreachable/failed setup; no destination-capacity-after-source-leg case; no actual `BotManager` replacement test; and no actor-disconnect case. The plan requires all of these categories (`.alice-supervision/active-plan.md:71-76`; implementation route `...implementation-plan-20260821.md:218-238`). `docs/HANDOVER.md:60-61` accurately admits the missing real HARD_PATH world and actor disconnect, but its claim that fixture covers "in-transit timeout/restart/abort/replacement" is materially too broad because only direct ledger state changes are tested, not the running task/command/guard behavior that contains the identified faults.

5. **The fixture's "survival" and removal checks only call public task callbacks directly, so they do not prove runtime lifecycle wiring.** `taskInterruptPolicies` constructs isolated ledgers and manually calls `survivalInterrupted` and `botRemoved` (`TransferFixture.java:189-212`). It does not pass through `SurvivalSystem`, `BotSession.tick`, death/removal, or validate bot-held state after a proven source leg. This is not the required in-transit hazard/removal proof and cannot support HANDOVER's broad coverage statement (`docs/HANDOVER.md:61`).
